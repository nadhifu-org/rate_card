Discovery questions

Ask these to scope the assessment
  1. Do you have an approved procurement policy? When was it last updated?
  2. Who can approve purchases, and up to what amount?
  3. Roughly how much do you spend with suppliers each year, and with how many suppliers?
  4. How many legal entities and locations buy goods or services?
  5. Where do you suspect you overpay, or where have prices crept up?
  6. Which contracts renew in the next 12 months, and who tracks them?
  7. How are savings reported today, and does finance confirm them?
  8. Can you export 12 months of transaction data? Who else needs to be involved?

Say it the way the landing page says it

  • Headline: “Procurement cost savings start with the right foundation.”
  • Promise: “Start with an assessment to identify gaps, prioritize improvements, and establish an evidence-based foundation for savings targets.”
  • Evidence: federal agencies using strategic sourcing reported 5–20% savings (U.S. GAO, GAO-13-408T, 2013).
  • Cost of waiting: “Left unaddressed, these gaps raise costs every month, on every purchase.”
  • Path: assess first; Tier 2 only if the policy or procedures need strengthening.
  • Fees: fixed, agreed in writing; never hourly, never a percentage of savings.
  • Who: senior expertise on every engagement; Dr. Paul Jilani leads it personally.
  • Trust: mutual NDA before confidential data is shared; leadership approves policies before adoption.
  • No savings figures on the call: any illustration goes in the proposal, built from the client’s addressable spend with documented assumptions and implementation requirements.
  • Never say: “guarantee”, “we will save you X%”, “lock in”. Realized savings depend on implementation, adoption, supplier conditions, and actual purchasing.

Public pricing: Tier 1 “starting at $5,000 USD”; Tier 2 “scoped following assessment”; retainers “optional monthly support”. Give Tier 2 and retainer figures on the call only when asked, as indicative.

Step 5

Quote summary

Fees are fixed for each tier’s included scope; never billed by the hour. If the prospect exceeds the included scope, quote the larger scope separately and in writing before work begins. Payments: Tier 1, 100% upfront; Tier 2, 50% at commencement, 30% at the draft milestone, 20% at completion. Retainers are optional, billed monthly in advance, start after the project, run an initial 3 months, then continue with 30 days’ notice; unused hours expire monthly. Travel, software, system configuration, legal review, and substantial data reconstruction are quoted separately. No guarantees, gain-share, success fees, or risk-sharing: savings targets are agreed after the assessment and baseline validation.
